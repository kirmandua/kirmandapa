# AI Prompt: Editorial / Brutalist Magazine Website Redesign

Style name: **Editorial Minimalist / Brutalist Magazine Design**

Copy and paste the prompt below into your AI design or coding tool.

---

## Prompt

Redesign the **visual style** of my website to match a bold, editorial magazine-style portfolio look — but do **NOT** change any text, content, copy, images, or page structure/information. Only restyle the visual presentation.

Apply the following design elements:

### Color Palette
- Mostly black, white, and off-white/cream backgrounds
- One bold mustard-yellow/amber accent color used for highlight blocks, buttons, or image backgrounds
- High contrast overall

### Typography
- Large, bold, lowercase sans-serif headlines (big and confident, like a magazine cover)
- Body text in a clean, minimal sans-serif font, small and understated
- Oversized decorative background text (words repeated or stretched across a section, low opacity or outline style) used as a graphic layout element behind content

### Layout Style
- Asymmetric editorial grid — mix of large image blocks, small text columns, and generous white space
- Avoid centered symmetrical layouts; make it feel like a fashion/photography magazine spread
- Alternate full-width black sections with white/cream sections for contrast

### Imagery
- Photos treated with high contrast or slight grain/duotone feel
- Cut-out or irregularly shaped image crops (not just plain rectangles) mixed with plain rectangular photo blocks
- Small circular or badge-style graphic elements (like a small circular logo or icon badge) layered near the top corner of sections

### Navigation & UI Elements
- Minimal top navigation with small text links, spaced out
- Buttons styled as simple bordered rectangles or pill shapes with small arrow icons
- Small dotted-circle or bullet accents used as decorative UI details (e.g., near stats or numbers)

### Overall Mood
Confident, artistic, high-fashion/photography-studio aesthetic — clean but bold, lots of negative space, strong typographic hierarchy, and one striking accent color against black-and-white.

---

## Important Constraint

> Keep all existing text, links, images, sections, and functionality exactly as they are — this is a visual/styling redesign only, not a content or structural change.
